# Amber Folio — feedback

Bug reports, questions and ideas for **[amberfolio.org](https://amberfolio.org)**,
the browser player for the Gold Box games.

[**Open an issue →**](https://github.com/amberfolio/amberfolio-feedback/issues/new/choose)

- **Something went wrong in the player** — use *Bug report*.
- **Your copy of the game is not recognised** — use *Copy not recognised*.
  The player shows the digests it could not match; paste those.
- **An idea or a question** — use *Idea or question*.

**Save files are welcome** — if a bug is about a particular save, attach
it (**Saves** on the player's toolbar exports one as a zip). **Never
attach the game's own files or your Adventurer's Journal**: they are the
publisher's, a public issue is public, and Amber Folio never needs them.
Describe what happened, paste the status line from the top right of the
player, and a screenshot of the site's own panels is welcome.

## House rules

This is a public space, and it has to stay on the right side of the
people who own the games. So, in any issue, comment or attachment:

- **No game files, manuals, maps or scans**, and no links to where they
  can be downloaded. That includes the Adventurer's Journal and the code
  wheel. Your saves are fine.
- **No requests for copies** of a game, and no help finding one outside
  the stores. Amber Folio plays the copy you bought; GOG and Steam both
  sell Pool of Radiance.
- **Nothing personal** — yours or anyone else's. Check a screenshot or a
  pasted path for your name before you post it.
- **Be decent to each other.** The [code of conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md)
  applies here.

**What happens if something breaks these rules:** the post or the
attachment is removed, and the thread may be locked. Anyone who posts
game files or download links a second time is blocked from the
repository. We check new issues within a couple of days.

**If you own the rights to something posted here** and want it taken
down, email **licensing@amberfolio.org**, or use
[GitHub's own takedown process](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/content-removal-policies/dmca-takedown-policy).

The emulator itself is open source at
[amberfolio/amberfolio](https://github.com/amberfolio/amberfolio) (AGPL-3.0);
this repository only tracks feedback about the website. For anything
private — licensing, legal, press — email **hello@amberfolio.org**.
